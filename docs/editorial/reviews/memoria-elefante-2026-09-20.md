## Fuentes abiertas

1. [Matriarchs as repositories of social knowledge in African elephants](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11313492/) — *Science* / PubMed; llamadas familiares y conocimiento social de matriarcas.
2. [The elephant brain in numbers](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4053853/) — *Frontiers in Neuroanatomy* / PMC; conteo neuronal de un cerebro africano.
3. [Extreme expansion of the olfactory receptor gene repertoire](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4158756/) — *Genome Research* / PMC; repertorio genómico olfativo.
4. [Aging: What We Can Learn From Elephants](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9261397/) — revisión de envejecimiento, vínculos y conocimiento.
5. [Building an Elephant Brain Database](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11693083/) — base post mortem de 23 individuos; no prueba memoria individual.
